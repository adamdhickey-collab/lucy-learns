-----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY EACH ONE HAS THE CONSTRAINTS IT HAS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SPLASH — the app paints a solid cream field (#f6e6d0) behind this art and
the art sits in a fixed 3:4 box in the middle of it. Two consequences:

  * The picture's own edges have to BE that cream. The reference has a
    green field running edge to edge across the bottom half; if that
    survives, the bottom of the art meets cream field in a visible seam
    and the whole thing reads as a photograph pasted onto paper.

  * It gets stretched to 3:4 on install. Portrait 2:3 is what the
    generator will give you, so the prompt asks for empty cream top and
    bottom that can be trimmed away without touching the figures.

ICON — three sizes get cut from this, down to 180px, and Android crops a
fourth to whatever shape the launcher wants, keeping only the middle 80%.
So: one subject, big, centred, nothing important near the edges. This is
why it is a crop of the reference rather than the whole scene — the full
composition at 180px is a dark smudge and a green smudge.

Using the attached illustration as the exact reference for character
design, palette and rendering style, redraw this scene as a PORTRAIT
composition.

STYLE — match the attached image exactly. Contemporary dog-training
instruction manual illustration. Simplified vector-like forms, crisp
edges, restrained detail. Mostly flat colour with subtle dimensional
shading. Muted green, teal, blue and charcoal palette. Warm,
approachable, premium, adult. Same linework weight and same rendering as
the reference.

SUBJECTS — keep both figures exactly as drawn in the reference:

Lucy — medium-large black Labrador / German Wirehaired Pointer mix,
glossy black coat, slight scruff at the muzzle, white blaze on the
chest, white toes on all four paws, soft floppy ears. She wears a flat
purple collar with a small round blue tag and NO harness. No white on
the face. Standing, alert, looking up at her handler.

Handler — the same woman: thirties, dark hair in a ponytail,
olive-green hoodie, dark navy jeans, grey sneakers, mustard-yellow treat
pouch at her right hip. Crouched at Lucy's level, in profile, one hand
low and open.

COMPOSITION — vertical portrait, the two figures stacked into a tall
frame: the crouching handler and Lucy facing each other, close together,
filling the middle of the image. Keep the eye contact between them — it
is the subject of the picture.

BACKGROUND — plain, flat, warm cream (#f6e6d0) filling the entire frame
edge to edge. NO grass field, NO horizon line, NO sky band, NO wall, no
scenery of any kind. Under the two figures put one soft muted-green
elliptical ground shape, like a rug of colour, floating clear of every
edge of the image with cream all around it. Add a soft contact shadow
where the feet and paws meet it.

DROP the squirrel entirely.

FRAMING — leave a generous band of empty cream above the handler's head
and below the ground shape, at least 12% of the image height at each
end, so the image can be cropped to 3:4 without cutting into the
figures. Nothing important in the top or bottom eighth.

Portrait orientation, 1024 x 1536. No text, no lettering, no logos, no
watermarks, no borders, no UI, no photorealism, no painterly texture, no
childish cartoon treatment.
